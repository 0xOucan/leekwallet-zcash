# Roadmap

The roadmap of LeekWallet Zcash, firmware included, is
[docs/zcash/ROADMAP.md](../docs/zcash/ROADMAP.md); what happened at each step
is in [docs/JOURNAL.md](../docs/JOURNAL.md).

Task numbers such as T11, T37 or T65 in comments and older documents refer to
upstream LeekWallet's roadmap, the Ethereum wallet this firmware was forked
from. It was removed from this tree; it is in the git history and in the
upstream repository, [0xoucan/leekwallet](https://github.com/0xoucan/leekwallet).
